#### en
She breathed life into him and said lovingly, "You are my son." The boy hugged her tight.

#### en
"Don't let anyone in until I finish my bath," said his mother. The boy stood bravely at the door.

#### te

#### hi
तभी शिव जी आए। बालक ने कहा, "मैंने माँ से वादा किया है — आप अंदर नहीं जा सकते।"
#### en
Then Shiva arrived. "I promised my mother — you cannot go in," said the boy.
### more
#### te
శివుడికి ఆ బాలుడు ఎవరో తెలియదు. బాలుడికీ శివుడు ఎవరో తెలియదు. ఇద్దరూ పట్టు వదల్లేదు. ఆ గొడవలో, ఒక్క క్షణంలో, బాలుడి తల పోయింది.
#### hi
शिव जी नहीं जानते थे कि बालक कौन है, और बालक भी नहीं जानता था कि वे कौन हैं। दोनों अड़े रहे। उस झगड़े में, एक ही पल में, बालक का सिर चला गया।
#### en
Shiva didn't know who the boy was, and the boy didn't know Shiva. Neither would give way. In that quarrel, in a single moment, the boy lost his head.

#### en
Parvati wept. Shiva was sorry. "Bring the head of the first creature you find," he said. They found a kind elephant.

#### en
They named him Ganapati, leader of all. It was the gift for his courage in keeping his word.

When you are brave and keep your word, Ganesha is with you.
