## title
1. Ganesha and the Moon

## beat 1
1. It was Ganesha's birthday!
2. Everyone brought modakas, and he ate until his tummy was round and full.

## beat 2
1. At night he rode home on his mouse.
2. The little mouse puffed and pulled.

## beat 3
1. A snake on the path!
2. The mouse jumped.
3. Ganesha tumbled, and the modakas rolled everywhere.

## beat 4
1. Up in the sky, the moon laughed and laughed.
2. "What a big tummy!" Ganesha stood up, gathered his modakas and asked calmly, "Is it kind to laugh when someone falls?"

## beat 5
1. "Go and hide," said Ganesha.
2. The moon slipped behind the clouds, and the sky went dark.

## beat 6
1. "I'm sorry!" said the moon.
2. Ganesha forgave him: "Each month you'll grow a little, then shrink a little." "Only on my birthday night, no one will look at you, to remember that day." That is why people don't look at the moon on Chavithi.

## beat 7
1. When someone falls, don't laugh, help them up.
2. And when you're wrong, say sorry.
