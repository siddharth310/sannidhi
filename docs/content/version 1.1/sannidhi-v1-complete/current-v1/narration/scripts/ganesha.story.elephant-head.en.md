## title
1. How Ganesha Got His Elephant Head

## beat 1
1. Mother Parvati shaped a little boy from turmeric paste.
2. He opened his eyes and smiled!
3. She breathed life into him and said lovingly, "You are my son." The boy hugged her tight.

## beat 2
1. "Don't let anyone in until I finish my bath," said his mother.
2. The boy stood bravely at the door.

## beat 3
1. Then Shiva arrived.
2. "I promised my mother, you cannot go in," said the boy.
3. Shiva didn't know who the boy was, and the boy didn't know Shiva.
4. Neither would give way.
5. In that quarrel, in a single moment, the boy lost his head.

## beat 4
1. Parvati wept.
2. Shiva was sorry.
3. "Bring the head of the first creature you find," he said.
4. They found a kind elephant.

## beat 5
1. The boy woke again with an elephant's head!
2. Shiva hugged him: "You will be worshipped before everyone." They named him Ganapati, leader of all.
3. It was the gift for his courage in keeping his word.

## beat 6
1. When you are brave and keep your word, Ganesha is with you.
