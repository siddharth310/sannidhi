Vināyaka Chavithi

Mon 14 Sep · Bhādrapada śukla chaturthī
Puja Story Chant Naivedyam

What makes it special

Celebrated as Ganesha's birthday — just as every task begins with him, the festival season begins with him too.

What families do

Families bring home a clay Ganapati, worship him with 21 kinds of leaves and offer modakas. Children place their books and pens in the puja. Many avoid looking at the moon that night.
