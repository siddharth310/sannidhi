## title
1. The Fruit of Wisdom

## beat 1
1. Sage Narada brought a golden mango to Kailasa.
2. "This is the fruit of wisdom," he said.

## beat 2
1. One fruit, two sons, Ganesha and Kartikeya.
2. "Whoever goes around the world first gets it," said Shiva.

## beat 3
1. Kartikeya leapt onto his peacock and flew off in a flash.
2. Ganesha looked at his little mouse and thought.

## beat 4
1. Ganesha walked around his parents three times and bowed.
2. "You are my whole world," he said.
3. Shiva and Parvati smiled.
4. Wisdom isn't about being fastest, it's knowing what truly matters.
5. The fruit went to Ganesha.

## beat 5
1. Kartikeya came back and felt a little sad.
2. His mother held him close: "You are our sweetest fruit." In Tamil, "Pazham nee", "you are the fruit." People say this became the name of Palani hill.

## beat 6
1. The people who love us are our whole world.
2. Stay close to them.
